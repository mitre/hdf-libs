## Schema

JUnit XML has no official schema: the format is defined by what the Ant/Maven/Jenkins tooling emits. Community XSDs exist (windyroad/JUnit-Schema `JUnit.xsd`; the Jenkins `junit-4.xsd` the JUnit 5 project uses for its own tests) and disagree with each other; none is vendored, because none is authoritative for the producers these fixtures come from (2026-09-20).
