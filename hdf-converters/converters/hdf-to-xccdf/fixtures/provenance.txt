and group, and the root `targets` array (the converter never reads it). Whole

redaction below.

(`/Users/<name>/.inspec/cache/<hash>/controls/SV-257781.rb`), now the

the scanned host's name.

Prose is untouched, which is the point of the fixture: multi-line default, check
and fix text, `code` ending in a newline, and result messages that open and close
with one. The schema-validation tests in both languages assert each of those
values comes back byte-exact (LF-normalized, since XML folds line endings).

The exporter's target-format schema, where one is vendored, lives under this converter's `schemas/` directory with its own `provenance.json`.
