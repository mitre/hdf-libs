## `expected/`

Generated by this repository's converters, not sourced. Regenerate with
`go test ./converters/hdf-to-csv/... -run TestSnapshots -update`; `minimal.csv`
regenerates with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 go test ./converters/hdf-to-csv/go/ -run TestGoldenParity`.

`minimal.csv` moved on 2026-09-24: SV-123457's Effective Impact went 0.00 to
0.50. Its `falsePositive` override carries a status and no impact, so the ladder
leaves the requirement's own 0.50 standing; the 0.00 came from the stored
`effectiveImpact` cache the column no longer reads. The stored field is left in
`minimal.json` on purpose — it is now the fixture's evidence that the cache is
ignored.

## Schema

The inputs are HDF documents; their schema is this repository's own (`hdf-schema`), and every input is validated by the converter tests through `hdf-validators`. The exporter's target-format schema, where one is vendored, lives under this converter's `schemas/` directory with its own `provenance.json`.
