## Schema

GitLab publishes a JSON Schema per report type and version (gitlab-org/security-products/security-report-schemas, `dist/<type>-report-format.json` at tag `v<version>`). The fixtures declare `version` 15.1.0 with `scan.type` sast (three) and dast (one), so `sast-report-format-15.1.0.json` and `dast-report-format-15.1.0.json` are vendored here and recorded in `provenance.json`.

These fixtures were authored in this repository (see above), so the fixture policy's validation requirement applies to them and is not met.
