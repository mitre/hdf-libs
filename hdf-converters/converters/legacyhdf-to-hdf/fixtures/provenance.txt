## `expected/`

Generated by this repository's converters, not sourced. Regenerate with
`go test ./converters/legacyhdf-to-hdf/... -run TestSnapshots -update`.

Four goldens here are over the ~1 MB fixture guideline (`wrapper` 2.8 MB, `ubi9-scan` 2.4 MB,
`container-scan` 2.4 MB, `three-layer-overlay` 1.6 MB; 9.5 MB together) and stay so by maintainer
decision in the 2026-09-14 fixture audit. Their inputs are not in this directory: they come from the
shared `@mitre/hdf-fixtures` corpus through a resolver, and `wrapper` alone has 58 other consumers, so
the input side cannot be trimmed from here. A digest-plus-summary assertion mode for just these four was
considered and rejected as a second mechanism not worth adding; a full golden gives a readable diff
naming the exact regressed field.
