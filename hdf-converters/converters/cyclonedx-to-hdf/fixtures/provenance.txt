## Schema

CycloneDX publishes JSON Schemas per `specVersion`. The fixtures use 1.4, 1.5 and 1.6; `bom-1.5.schema.json` and `bom-1.6.schema.json` are vendored here with the `spdx.schema.json` and `jsf-0.82.schema.json` they reference, recorded in `provenance.json`; `bom-1.4.schema.json` is already vendored at `hdf-to-cyclonedx-vex/schemas/` and is not copied twice.

Validation (gojsonschema, 2026-09-20): five of the six inputs validate against the schema for their `specVersion`. `spdx-to-cyclonedx.json` does not — 7 errors: two `metadata.authors[].email` are empty strings, which fail `idn-email`, and three `components[0].evidence.licenses[]` entries carry only license `text` where the 1.5 schema requires an `id` or `name`. It is kept as-is: it is verbatim heimdall2 sample data produced by a real SPDX→CycloneDX conversion, and the converter has to survive output like it.
